# Overview

The RIPPLE Social Presence & Marketing System is a business support system designed to establish, maintain, and grow RIPPLE's online presence.

The goal is to create a consistent and sustainable approach for:

- Brand communication
- Social media presence
- Content creation
- Customer engagement
- Local business visibility

This system operates independently from the RIPPLE website workflow while sharing the same brand identity and business information.

---

# Vision

Create a simple, repeatable marketing workflow that allows RIPPLE to:

- Present products professionally
- Build customer trust
- Showcase handmade craftsmanship
- Maintain consistent communication
- Grow local awareness

The system should support the business without creating unnecessary complexity.

---

# Principles

## Keep It Human

RIPPLE is a handmade business. Marketing should highlight:

- Craftsmanship
- Personal stories
- Behind-the-scenes work
- Authentic customer relationships

Automation should assist, not replace, the human element.

---

## Keep It Simple

The system should:

- Use free or low-cost tools where possible
- Avoid unnecessary complexity
- Require minimal maintenance
- Support gradual growth

---

## Separate Systems, Shared Identity

The marketing system is independent from the website system.

Website:
- Product catalog
- Customer experience
- Orders
- Information

Marketing:
- Awareness
- Engagement
- Community building
- Promotion

# Phase 1 — Social Presence Migration

## Goal

Transition existing online presence from Honeycomb Arts and Crafts to RIPPLE Bakes & Makes.

## Tasks

### Facebook

- Audit existing page
- Review followers, reviews, and historical content
- Rename page
- Update profile image
- Update cover image
- Update description
- Update categories
- Create transition announcement post

### Instagram

- Evaluate existing account options
- Create or update account
- Align branding
- Connect website

## Deliverable

Consistent RIPPLE social identity.
